We can also use LLM to generate detailed reports to validate the decisions

## Model responsibility split

The deterministic calculations and hard business rules should remain outside the LLM. Python will calculate GCC audience percentage, GCC reachable audience, engagement rate, likes-to-comments ratio, commercial-content rate, anomaly rules, volatility, weighted score, and the final ONBOARD / HOLD / PASS decision.

The LLMs will be used only for interpretation, classification, and explanation.

For caption-level analysis, I can use Qwen3-14B. It will classify commercial intent, identify CTAs, discount codes, restock language, product reviews, unboxing content, and product education. I will ignore regex-based detection and require the model to return structured JSON instead.

For brand and category analysis, I will use an LLM because maintaining a complete brand dictionary is outside the scope of this prototype. Gemini is suitable for this stage because brand information and catalog positioning may change over time. If current information is important, Gemini should use a grounded or trusted catalog source rather than relying only on model memory.

## Model assignment

Qwen3-14B:
- Caption classification
- Commercial-intent extraction
- CTA detection
- Discount-code detection
- Restock and unboxing detection
- Product-review classification
- Structured evidence extraction

Gemini Flash:
- Brand classification
- Product-category classification
- Prestige-tier assessment
- Boutiqaat catalog-fit analysis
- Current brand-information verification

A larger reasoning model such as Gemini Pro or Qwen3-32B:
- Final explanation
- Cross-signal reasoning
- Conflict resolution
- Diagnostic recommendation report

## Why the final explanation needs a stronger model

The final explanation is more complex than individual caption classification because it must reason across many parameters at the same time.

It must understand trade-offs such as:
- High GCC relevance but suspicious engagement
- Strong luxury fit but weak commercial intent
- Small audience but unusually strong interaction quality
- High promotional activity but poor Boutiqaat category fit
- Large follower count but low GCC reach

Therefore, the final report should be generated by a larger reasoning model with stronger multi-parameter reasoning. The model should receive the already-calculated metrics, weighted score, hard-rule results, anomaly flags, and extracted evidence.

The larger model should explain and defend the decision, but it should not calculate or override the score. The score and ONBOARD / HOLD / PASS decision must remain deterministic and reproducible in Python.

## Final hybrid flow

Raw profile and post data
        ↓
Classical preprocessing and validation
        ↓
Qwen3-14B caption interpretation
        ↓
Gemini brand and category verification
        ↓
Classical feature aggregation and anomaly detection
        ↓
Deterministic weighted score and decision
        ↓
Larger reasoning model generates the final explanation


This architecture uses smaller or faster models for repetitive extraction, a model with broader current knowledge for brand verification, and a larger reasoning model for the final multi-parameter explanation.
